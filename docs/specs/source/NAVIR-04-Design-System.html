How to use this document

  • Every colour, size and radius is given as a number you can copy (section 10 repeats them all as CSS variables).
  • Components are shown rendered with the real classes (for example .btn, .chip, .pill-status), so the picture and the code always match.
  • To reuse in another project: keep sections 1 to 5 and 7 to 9 as the rules, replace the brand colours and fonts in sections 2 and 3, and re-render section 6 with the new tokens.

1. Brand principles and voice

NAVIR looks like a calm, clean clinic that speaks like a friendly friend: few elements, plenty of space, one confident blue.

( navir )

Simple habit. Own your face.

Face and body care made for men. Lightweight, non-greasy, and just three steps a day.

PrincipleWhat it means in the product
CalmPale blue-grey background, white cards, navy actions. One strong element per area; no gradients or shadows inside the shop (only on the dark hero).
SimpleThree steps (Cleanse, Treat, Protect), four quiz questions, one primary button per screen.
Masculine, not harshNavy and steel blue, a serif display face for headings, square-ish corners (10 px).
TrustworthyPlain numbers (prices, stock, ratings), verified-buyer reviews, clear order status, no dark patterns.
Phone firstBig tap targets (46 px primary buttons), one column on phones, nothing scrolls sideways.

Errors explain the fix, never blame."Sorry, this product is out of stock." · "Please answer: Shave or beard?"

3. Typography

A serif for voice (headings, product names, prices), a sans for work (everything you read and click).

Instrument Serif

Headings, product names, prices, step titles. Weight 400 only, tight line height, letter-spacing -0.01em. CSS: var(--serif), fallback Georgia.

Inter

Body, buttons, forms, tables, labels. Weights 400, 500, 600. CSS: var(--sans), fallback system-ui.

7. Patterns

How components combine for the situations every page meets.

Empty states

Your cart is empty.

Start with a cleanser, step one of your daily routine.

Shop now
No orders yet.

When you place an order, you can follow it here.

Shop now

Empty state = white card, serif sentence, one muted line that says what to do, one primary button. For a search with no result use a muted sentence with a link: "Nothing matches “shampoo”. Try another word, or see all products."

Two-step delete

Deletethen, in the same place:Yes, delete

Destructive actions never happen on the first click. The button turns into a confirmation in place (no modal) and the result is reported in an alert.

Guest to customer

Anyone can browse and fill the cart. An account is requested only at checkout; after log in or sign up the visitor returns to checkout with the cart intact. Never ask for an account before the visitor has a reason to give one.

Forms

  • Pre-fill what we know (name, email) and show read-only values as plain muted text.
  • One column on phones; two short fields may share a row on desktop.
  • Validate on the server; show one red alert at the top and keep what the user typed.
  • The page's single primary button is the full-width last element of the form.

Feedback after an action

8. Accessibility

RuleHow the shop does it
ContrastBody text 15:1 or better; secondary text at least 4.7:1; white on navy 14.5:1 (section 2). Gold stars (2.9:1) are always paired with a number.
Never colour aloneStatus pills carry the word; steps carry numbers and ✓; errors carry text.
LabelsEvery input has a visible label; icon-only buttons have aria-label (Open menu, Remove, Log out); decorative icons are aria-hidden.
KeyboardAll controls are reachable by Tab; Escape closes the phone menu; the star picker and quiz options are real radio inputs with a visible focus outline.
StructureOne H1 per page, then headings in order; the main menu is a nav with a label; the current step uses aria-current.
TouchPrimary buttons 46 px, small buttons 34 px, tap rows 52 px; at least 8 px between tap targets.
ImagesProduct photos have the product name as alt text; decorative images have none.
MotionOnly small slide and fade effects (about 0.25 s). Nothing flashes or auto-plays.
Zoom and reflowLayout works at 375 px and when text is enlarged; no sideways scrolling.

9. Do and don't

Do
  • Use --navy for the one main action on a page.
  • Keep white cards on the --bg page.
  • Use the serif for names, prices and headings only.
  • Show status as a word plus colour.
  • Say what will happen next, in a short sentence.
  • Test every page at 375 px wide.
  • Confirm destructive actions in place.
Don't
  • Put two primary buttons side by side.
  • Add new colours; map to a token or a status colour.
  • Use the serif for long text or small labels.
  • Rely on colour (or gold stars) alone.
  • Use a placeholder as the only label.
  • Add shadows or gradients inside the shop.
  • Hide an important link only on phones without a replacement.
