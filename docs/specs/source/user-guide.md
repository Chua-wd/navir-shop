## 1. Getting started

NAVIR is an online shop for men's skincare. You can browse products, take a short quiz that builds your daily routine, order, pay by bank transfer and follow your order. The shop team uses the same site to manage orders, products and users.

**The shop:** navir.site.je (works on phones and computers).

**Four kinds of user**

| You are | You can | How to get access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Visitor (not logged in) | Browse, search, take the quiz, fill a cart | Nothing needed |
| Shopper (role: User) | Everything above + order, pay, track orders, review products you bought | Sign up (free) |
| Admin | Everything a shopper can + dashboard, orders, products, categories | Given by a System Admin |
| System Admin | Everything an admin can + user management | The shop owner |

**Try it with the demo accounts.** On the Log in page, the **"Try the demo"** box has two ready-made accounts; click one to fill the form, then press Log in. Both use the password shown in the box.

| Demo account | What it shows | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shopper (`demo.user@navir.test`) | Shopping, ordering, tracking, reviews | Can't change its own name, email or password |
| Admin (`demo.admin@navir.test`) | The whole admin area | Can look at everything and move an order's status, but can't save other changes |

## 2. For shoppers

### 2.1 Browse and search

1. Open **Shop** in the top menu. Products show their photo, category, name, price and, when people have reviewed them, stars.
2. Use the **sort tabs** (Newest, Price: low to high, Price: high to low, Top rated) and the **category links** to narrow the list.
3. Type a word in **Search products** (for example "serum") and press Enter. It looks in names, descriptions and categories, and works together with the sort and category you chose.
4. Click a product to open its page: photos, size, stock, description, reviews and related products. **← All products** takes you back to the full list.

### 2.2 Find my routine (skin quiz)

Not sure what to buy? The quiz recommends a 3-step routine (Cleanse, Treat, Protect) from NAVIR products. No photos and no sign-up.

1. Open **Find my routine** in the menu (or the card on the home page).
2. Answer the 4 questions: how your skin feels after washing, what you want to fix, how long you're outside, and whether you shave or have a beard.
3. Press **Show my routine**.
4. Read each step: the product, why it was chosen and its price. Press **Add to cart** on one product, or **Add the whole routine to cart** for all of them.
5. Under **Good extras for you** you may see shaving foam, beard oil or a toner. The link in your browser's address bar can be shared; it opens the same result.

![The quiz questions](shot:s05-quiz)

![A routine result with "Add the whole routine to cart"](shot:s06-quiz-result)

The quiz is a general guide, not medical advice. If you have a skin condition, ask a dermatologist first.

### 2.3 Add to cart and check your cart

1. On a product page choose the quantity with **−** and **+**, then press **Add to cart**. A small popup confirms it and the **Cart** number in the menu goes up.
2. Open **Cart**. Change quantities with − and +, or remove a product with **×**. The **+** is greyed out when you reach the stock limit.
3. The summary shows subtotal, shipping and total. Shipping is **Rp 15,000**, or **free from Rp 200,000**; a bar tells you how much more you need.
4. You don't need an account to fill the cart. Press **Continue to checkout** when you're ready.

![The cart with the order summary](shot:s07-cart)

### 2.4 Checkout

1. If you're not logged in, you'll be asked to **log in or sign up**. After that you come straight back to checkout and your cart is still there.
2. Fill in **recipient name, phone / WhatsApp, email and full address**. Name and email are pre-filled from your account; the note is optional.
3. Check **your order** on the right, then press **Place order**.
4. If a product ran out of stock in the meantime, nothing is saved and a message tells you which one. Go back to the cart and adjust.

![Checkout: shipping details and order summary](shot:s08-checkout)

### 2.5 Pay by bank transfer and confirm

After **Place order** you land on the payment page and your order number (like NVR-7KQ2M9XA) is created.

1. Transfer the **exact amount** to the bank account shown. **Copy account number** copies the digits for your banking app.
2. In **Confirmation form**, the **Message** box is already filled with your order number, items, total and address. Add anything you like, for example the time of transfer.
3. Press **Send confirmation**. You'll see a thank-you page with **Track this order**.
4. The shop checks the transfer in its bank account and updates your order. Please transfer within 24 hours.

If you close the page before sending the confirmation, open **My orders**, open the order and press **Send payment details**.

![The payment page with the pre-filled message](shot:s09-payment)

![Thank-you page](shot:s10-done)

### 2.6 Track your orders

1. Open **My orders** in the top menu. Orders are listed newest first, with photos, date, number of items, status and total.
2. Click an order to see where it is. The tracker moves through **Order placed → Processing → Shipped → Delivered**. A returned order shows a notice instead.
3. The page also shows your items, totals, shipping address and whether your payment confirmation was sent.

You can only see your own orders.

![My orders](shot:s11-my-orders)

![An order with its status tracker](shot:s12-order-detail)

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Pending | We have your order and are waiting to confirm your payment |
| Processing | Payment confirmed; the order is being packed |
| Shipped | On its way to you |
| Delivered | It arrived |
| Returned | The order was returned |

### 2.7 Write a review

1. Open the page of a product you have **ordered**, then scroll to **Reviews**.
2. Click the stars (1 to 5), add an optional comment and press **Post review**.
3. Your review shows your first name and last initial, with a **Verified buyer** tag. To change it, post again: it updates your earlier review. You can remove your own review with **Remove**.

If you haven't bought the product, the box says only customers who ordered it can review it. This keeps reviews honest.

## 3. For admins

Log in with an Admin or System Admin account. After log in you land on the **Dashboard**. The left menu shows **Dashboard, Orders, Products, Product Categories** (and **User Management** for System Admins), plus **Profile**. Shortcuts at the bottom: **Add product**, **Low stock** and **View shop**.

### 3.1 Dashboard

The dashboard answers "how is the shop doing?" for the last **7, 30 or 90 days** (switch at the top right).

- **Summary cards:** products, categories, product clicks, orders and revenue, each compared with the period before. Revenue does not count returned orders.
- **Conversion gauge:** orders divided by product-page visits, against a 5% goal.
- **Charts and lists:** clicks or revenue over time, clicks by category, most-clicked products, order fulfilment by status, average order value, recent orders and a low-stock count (products with fewer than 10).

![The admin dashboard](shot:s20-dashboard)

### 3.2 Handle orders

1. Open **Orders**. The number next to it counts orders still **Pending**.
2. Use the tabs (**All, Pending, Processing, Shipped, Delivered, Returned**) or the search box (order number, name or email) to find an order.
3. The **Payment** column says **Waiting** until the customer sends the confirmation, then **Confirmation sent**.
4. Click an order number. You see the items, totals, the customer's **payment message**, the shipping address, phone, email and note.
5. **Check your bank account** for the transfer. Then choose the new status in **Status** and press **Update**. The order moves Pending → Processing → Shipped → Delivered, and the customer sees it under My orders.

![The orders list with status tabs and search](shot:s21-orders)

![One order: items, payment message, address and status](shot:s22-order-detail)

### 3.3 Manage products

1. Open **Products** for the table (10 per page). Use **Search products** in the left menu, or **Low stock** to see products under 10.
2. **+ Add product** opens the form: category (optional), name, description, price and stock (whole numbers), and a photo (JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 2 MB).
3. **Edit** changes any of this. You can replace the photo, or tick **Remove image** to show the "No photo yet" placeholder.
4. **Delete** asks you to confirm with **Yes, delete**; the photo file is deleted too. Customers' past orders for that product are removed with it, so prefer setting stock to 0 for products you only stop selling.

![The product list](shot:s23-products)

![Editing a product](shot:s24-product-edit)

### 3.4 Manage categories

Open **Product Categories**. Each category shows how many products it has. Add, rename or delete categories; names must be unique and at most 100 characters. Deleting a category keeps its products: they become "Uncategorized".

![Categories](shot:s25-categories)

On a phone the admin menu folds behind the **☰** button at the top. Tap it to open the menu, tap again to close it.

## 4. For system admins: user management

Only System Admins see **User Management**.

1. Open **User Management**. Each row shows the person's name, email, role and a trash icon. Search by name or email, filter by role, and click **Name** to sort A–Z or Z–A.
2. **Change a role:** pick **User, Admin or System Admin** in the dropdown. It saves immediately and a green message confirms it.
3. **Delete a user:** click the trash icon, then **Yes, delete**. This also deletes that person's orders and reviews.
4. Your own row is marked **you**: you can't change your own role or delete yourself, so the shop never ends up without a System Admin.

| Role | What it unlocks |
| --- | --- |
| User | Shop, quiz, cart, checkout, My orders, profile, reviews |
| Admin | + Dashboard, Orders, Products, Product Categories |
| System Admin | + User Management |

## 5. FAQ and troubleshooting

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| I can't write a review. | Reviews are for customers who ordered the product (and the order wasn't returned). Log in with the account that placed the order. |
| "Only admins can open this page" (403). | Your role doesn't include that page. Ask a System Admin to change your role. |
| I lost my password. | On the Log in page choose **Forgot password?** and follow the link sent to your email. |
| Why do I have to log in at checkout? | The order is saved under your account so you can track it later. Your cart is kept. |
| I paid but my order still says Pending. | Pending means the shop hasn't confirmed the transfer yet. Make sure you pressed **Send confirmation** (open the order and use **Send payment details** if not). |
| The "Add to cart" button says Out of stock. | That product has no stock left for now. |
| My cart number disappeared. | The cart is kept for a limited time per browser. Add the products again. |
| The demo account says it can't save changes. | Demo accounts are shared and read-only. Sign up for your own account to change a profile. |
| A quiz recommendation has a different product than before. | Recommendations use the products available now; if one was renamed or removed, a similar one from the same category is used. |

## 6. Quick reference

| Task | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Search for a product | Shop → Search products |
| Get a routine | Find my routine |
| See your cart | Cart (top bar) |
| Check out | Cart → Continue to checkout |
| Send payment confirmation | After Place order, or My orders → order → Send payment details |
| Follow an order | My orders |
| Review a product | Product page → Reviews (after you bought it) |
| Handle an order (admin) | Admin → Orders → order → Status → Update |
| Add or edit a product (admin) | Admin → Products |
| Change someone's role (System Admin) | Admin → User Management |

**Rules to remember:** shipping is Rp 15,000, free from Rp 200,000 · stock is checked when you place the order · one review per product per customer · demo accounts can't save changes (except placing orders, reviews and moving an order's status).
